Checkpoint

A game library and playtime tracker. Keep track of what you play, how long you play it, and what you think of it.

What you can do

  • Build your library. Search for games and add them with a status: playing, completed, on hold, dropped or planned.
  • Log your hours. Record play sessions with a platform and a note, and see your totals and your week at a glance.
  • Rate and favourite. Give games a star rating and pin up to four favourites to your profile.
  • Import from Steam. Connect your Steam ID to bring in your owned games and playtime, and load achievements per game.
  • Follow other players. Find people by name or username and follow them.

Signing in

You can browse and search without an account. To build a library you sign in with your Google account or with an email and password. Checkpoint only asks Google for your basic profile (name, email address and profile picture) to create your account. It does not access any other Google data.

Your data

Your library, ratings, hours and play logs are private to you. Other signed-in members can only see your display name, username and avatar. Read the full Privacy Policy to see exactly what is stored and how to delete it.
