Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Checkpoint is a game library and playtime tracker. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can have it deleted. In this policy, "Checkpoint", "we" and "us" mean the developer of the Checkpoint app.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier. We request only the basic profile scopes (openid, email, profile). If you sign up with an email and password instead, we store your email address and a password hash handled by our authentication provider.
  • Your profile. Your display name, username, bio and avatar. Your avatar is the picture you upload, or the one from your Google account.
  • Your library. The games you add and, for each one, your status, rating, hours played, purchase price, favourite flag and the platform you played on, plus any play sessions and notes you log.
  • Steam information (optional). If you connect Steam, we store the Steam ID you enter. We use it to fetch your owned games and playtime, how many achievements you have unlocked in each game you played, and a game's full list of achievements when you or another signed-in member opens that game's achievements on your profile. Other signed-in members can see your Steam ID (it is the link on your profile's Steam icon). After you have imported your library once, we also refresh it automatically in the background about twice a day while you are actively using the app, so your playtime stays current. This works only if your Steam profile's game details are public.
  • Connected services (optional). Which of PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Discord, Instagram and Twitch you have marked as connected, and the PSN id, Xbox gamertag and the Discord, Instagram and Twitch usernames you enter for them (Steam is covered above). Other signed-in members can see them: they are what the icons above the bio on your profile open.
  • Social activity. Which members you follow.
  • Search terms. When you search for a game, the search term is sent to our server so it can look the game up.
  • On your device. The app keeps your sign-in session, your recent searches and a saved copy of your library and profile so it works offline.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the app: to sign you in, show your library and statistics, import your Steam games, let you find and follow other members, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and the app does not include advertising or analytics tools.

Who can see what

  • Private to you: your email address, the purchase price you enter for a game, and the notes you write on play sessions.
  • Visible to other signed-in members: your display name, username, avatar, bio and the month you joined; your library (each game with its status, your rating, hours played, the platform you played on and whether it is a favourite) and so the totals made from it; your latest play sessions (the game, hours, platform and when, without notes); who follows whom; and the accounts you connected: your Steam ID, PSN id, Xbox gamertag and Discord, Instagram and Twitch usernames. Your Steam achievement progress (how many you unlocked in each game) is shown on your profile, and a game's achievements are shown when a member opens that game there.
  • Nothing in this list is visible to people who are not signed in.
  • Your avatar image is stored in a public file bucket, so anyone with its link can view it.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication, file storage and server functions.
  • Google provides "Sign in with Google".
  • IGDB (a Twitch service) provides game information. Our server sends it your search terms; it does not receive your identity.
  • Steam Web API (Valve) receives your Steam ID when you import your library and when it is refreshed in the background.

Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy policy. We share data with them only as needed to provide the features above.

Google user data

Checkpoint uses your Google account only to sign you in and to fill in your name, email address and profile picture. We do not read or store any other Google data, and we do not share Google user data with third parties except the service providers listed above that run the app. Checkpoint's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app, under Settings > Account > Delete account, or by emailing us at the address below. Deleting it removes your profile, library, logs, follows, uploaded avatar and sign-in. Games you added to the shared game catalog remain available to other members, but are no longer linked to you.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing the address below. You can edit your profile, remove games from your library and delete your account inside the app at any time. You can also remove Checkpoint's access to your Google account in your Google Account settings under "Sign in with Google".

Children

Checkpoint is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
